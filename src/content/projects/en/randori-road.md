A man in black came and took down the dojo's sign. To get it back, you have to climb all the way up to him, from the regional tournament to the world championship.

## The idea

- **Every card is a judo move**: grips, off-balancing (kuzushi), throws, counters, groundwork.
- **No off-balancing, no real throw**: just like on the mat, you have to set your opponent up before attacking.
- **Your tokui-waza sets the style of the run**: legs, hips, arms, counters, ground, sacrifices or tactics, each with its own rule.
- **A season in three acts**, with two possible finals per act and eight paths to discover.
- **A technique notebook**: entries about real judo techniques.

## Where it stands

The game engine, the full season and meta-progression are in place. The game will be released for free on iOS.
